How this map works

Panel
“Close panel” gives the whole screen to the map; “Show panel” (or “Show stops”) on the map brings it back.
Routes
Planned walks in visit order. Tap a route to see its stops, then tap a stop for details. Lines are straight, not the street path; “Walk it in Google Maps” gives directions.
Explore
Every place, coloured by kind, with no routes. Narrow them with the filters, then “Make walks” chains what is left into walks within your limits. “From a point I pick” builds one walk around where you tap.
Events
Exhibitions, shows and fairs. Pick a date and a time; faded pins are not on then.
Your marks
Star a must-see; Fade a place you don’t want (faded places are left out of new walks). At the bottom of each card, pick a rating and press Visited to keep it. The Visited and faded settings in each tab show, fade or hide those places. Marks stay in this browser only.
